perp-vault-guard

A USDC vault on Solana whose manager can trade perps, but only inside limits the owner sets on chain. The manager never holds the funds or the perp account key. The vault PDA signs every order, and only after the policy checks pass.

About the mock

Drift v2's devnet deployment does not accept deposits or orders right now: its market accounts were never migrated (SpotMarket 776 bytes on chain vs 808 in the IDL, PerpMarket 1216 vs 1240), so every deposit fails with 6087 and every order with 6078. The repo includes a labelled mock with Drift's interface for the four instructions the vault calls: same names, account order, arg layout, PDA seeds and oracle offset. The vault program is the same build either way and points at Drift on mainnet. The SOL price is read live from Drift's devnet market.
